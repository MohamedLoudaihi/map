A LITTLE CLARITY

Know before you go.

Reported prices from GasMap

Fuel prices are community-reported prices from GasMap, not live pump sensor readings. Every price has a source and its available timestamp. “Source updated” indicates the page update time when a fuel-specific report date is unavailable. A refreshed feed can still contain older prices; use “Price date within” to choose your freshness limit. Always confirm the price at the station.

Checking the latest available price reports…

More stations from OpenStreetMap

Additional station names, locations, and addresses come from community maintained OpenStreetMap data. Some stations or details may be missing or out of date. A station without a price can still be selected for directions.

Cash and member prices differ

Cash prices usually apply without a membership. Member prices may require a loyalty card, app, payment method, or other conditions. When a source does not specify a payment type, the report is marked “Payment not specified.” Cash or member filters hide those reports. Prices are in yen per litre.

Your route, with Google Maps

Select a station to load its map. “Get directions” opens Google Maps with that station as your destination. “Near me” uses your location, with your browser’s permission, to sort by distance; it is not uploaded to our own server.

Your saved stops stay with you

Saved stations are stored in this browser. They do not sync between devices. If you clear browser storage, your saved stations will be cleared too.
